The marker in motion

Four ways for the marker to say "in use", each running live at full size, with its timing written under it. All four stop and go gray the moment you take the mouse, and stay still when Plasma's animations are turned off (System Settings, Animation speed). None flashes faster than once a second.

A · On air. The whole light breathes, like a lit ON AIR sign.
  1. 0 to 1.2 s: the core brightens and a soft glow spreads around the ring.
  2. 1.2 to 2.4 s: both ease back. Repeats while Claude holds control.
B · Recording. A ring leaves the core and fades, like a recording light's pulse.
  1. 0 s: a thin ring starts at the core's edge.
  2. 0 to 1.4 s: it grows past the marker and fades out.
  3. 2 s: the next one. The core stays still.
C · REC. The core blinks like a camera's REC light.
  1. 1.2 s on, 0.6 s dim, no fade between.
  2. The ring stays still.
D · Live. Lit and still while Claude only holds control; one ripple each time Claude clicks, types or scrolls, so the light shows use, not just permission.
  1. Each action: the core flashes slightly brighter and one ring leaves it over 0.9 s.
  2. Between actions: steady. Here, the actions are simulated at random.
D · Live, again, beside E to compare: each action sends one ring out from the core.
E · Live, inward. The same as D, but each action draws a ring in: it starts just outside the marker and closes on the core over 0.9 s, picking up speed; the core brightens as it lands.
  1. 0 to 0.2 s: the ring fades in, just outside the marker.
  2. 0.2 to 0.9 s: it closes in, speeding up, and meets the core's edge.
  3. 0.9 s: the core brightens briefly, then settles. Between actions: steady.
Paused, for all four. You took the mouse: the motion stops and the marker goes gray.
